Section 8 housing in Phoenix, AZ

How the Housing Choice Voucher
program works

The Housing Choice Voucher program, commonly called Section 8, is funded by HUD and run locally by housing authorities. It helps eligible families afford safe, decent housing in the private rental market.

In most cases the housing authority pays a portion of the rent directly to the owner, and the family pays the rest. Before move-in, the home must pass a Housing Quality Standards (HQS) inspection.

SEGO Estates specializes in Section 8 rental homes across the Phoenix Metro area. We know the program, so you don’t have to figure it out alone.

What to have ready
  • Your voucher and recent paperwork from your housing authority (voucher holders)
  • Photo ID for adult members of your household
  • Household size and your ideal move-in timing
  • Your housing authority caseworker’s contact details
  • Your questions, we’re glad to answer them

Requirements vary by housing authority and by property. We’ll walk you through exactly what’s needed for your situation.
