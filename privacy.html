1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may enter:

  • Contact and rental inquiries: name, email, optional phone number, inquiry type, message and newsletter choice.
  • Consultation requests: contact details, area of interest, capital range, investment timeline, preferred date and time, and the background you describe.
  • Accounts: name, email, optional phone number, account type (client or tenant), password and your agreement to our Terms.
  • Resident and client tools: maintenance requests (property address, category, urgency, access time and description), referrals, portal messages, settings, and banking details if you enter them in the direct deposit form.

2. How information reaches us

This website does not run its own server or user database.

  • The contact, consultation, newsletter, maintenance, new account and account deletion features open a drafted email in your own email application. We receive that information only if you send the email. It is addressed to sego.institutional@gmail.com, and some messages also copy santiago@segocapital.com.
  • Account details, settings and saved form entries are stored in your web browser on your device.
  • Referrals and portal messages typed into the dashboard are not transmitted to us. To reach us, please email.
  • A photo or video selected on the maintenance form is not uploaded or attached automatically. Attach it to the email yourself if you want us to receive it.
  • We also receive anything else you send us directly.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and local or session storage in your browser for:

  • Essential functions: account sign-in and remembering your cookie choice.
  • Preferences: theme, language, accessibility settings and saved contact form entries.
  • Newsletter and account setup: remembering the email you entered.

Cookies, which can include your name, email, phone number and account type, are sent by your browser to our hosting provider when pages load. Your password is kept in your browser's local storage in an encoded form that is not strong encryption, so use a password you do not use anywhere else. Banking details entered in the direct deposit form are encrypted before being saved in your browser, but the key is stored on the same device.

You can change optional choices through "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the website, or clear your browser's site data to remove everything stored on your device. We do not currently run analytics or advertising tools.

4. How we use information

  • Answer inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Review rental interest, handle maintenance requests and serve residents.
  • Communicate with clients and prospective investors.
  • Send updates you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.
  • Operate and protect the website, and meet legal obligations, including fair housing laws.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information may be shared:

  • With providers that make the website and our email work, as described below.
  • With housing authorities, contractors or professional advisers when needed to handle your request or tenancy.
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety and property.
  • As part of a merger, reorganization or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

6. Third-party services

These services may receive technical data such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages (website hosting).
  • Google Fonts (typefaces).
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (the charting library used in dashboards).
  • Google Drive (library documents you choose to open).
  • Email providers, including Google (Gmail), when you email us.

7. Google account data

This website does not currently request access to data from your Google account. Opening a library document takes you to Google Drive, where Google handles your sign-in and may show us, as the document owner, information about who accessed or requested the file.

If we add a Google sign-in or other Google integration, we will update this policy first to describe what is accessed and how it is used, stored and shared. Our use of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and security

We keep information sent to us for as long as needed for the purpose it was sent, and as required for business and legal records. Data stored in your browser stays until you delete it, it expires or your browser removes it. We take reasonable care with information, but no website, device or email system is completely secure. Please do not send Social Security numbers or banking details by email.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, receive a copy of or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing santiago@segocapital.com or sego.institutional@gmail.com. We respond to data and deletion requests within 5 business days. We may need to verify your identity, and may keep records the law requires. You may have additional rights depending on where you live.

Signing out does not erase information stored in your browser. To erase it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

10. Children

The website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
